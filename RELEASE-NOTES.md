An unsigned iPhone build for signing and sideloading through AltStore Classic. Download TabLockdown-unsigned.ipa and import it into AltStore on your iPhone.

Includes a native browser, hostname lockdown, popup blocking even when unlocked, current-page reload, local bookmarks, and on-device video detection with a separate native player.

The app has no developer-operated backend or metered service. Each phone connects directly to the websites it visits.

Free Apple-account installations require refreshing every seven days. Physical iPhone installation and site-specific streaming compatibility require device testing. DRM and source access restrictions still apply.

Windows installation instructions are at https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-classic/how-to-install-altstore-windows
